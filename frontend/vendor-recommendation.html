Recommendation Algorithm & Scoring Methodology

VEPRS employs a multi-criteria decision model. Individual criteria scores (0–100) are synthesized into a normalized overall composite score using weighted linear aggregation:

Overall Score = (Price Score × 0.40) + (Quality Score × 0.35) + (Delivery Score × 0.25)
💰 Price Criterion (40%)
Evaluates quotation unit price, cost efficiency, and discount terms.
⭐ Quality Criterion (35%)
Assesses product specifications, warranty coverage, and hardware durability.
🚚 Delivery Criterion (25%)
Measures delivery turnaround lead times, logistics tracking, and fulfillment reliability.
